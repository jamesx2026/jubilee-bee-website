Our Story

Jubilee Bee Event Rentals was founded with a simple mission: to bring unexpected joy and lasting memories to life's most special celebrations. We believe every wedding should have that one element that makes guests say "I can't believe they had THAT!"

Our modern claw machines do exactly that. They're not just entertainment — they're conversation starters, photo opportunities, and guaranteed laughter generators. From grandmother to flower girl, everyone can't resist trying their luck at winning the perfect plush prize.

Based in Vancouver, we proudly serve the Lower Mainland and beyond, bringing our buzzing excitement to weddings throughout the region.
